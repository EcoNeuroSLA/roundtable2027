<h3>Travel and Lodging</h3>

<h4>Air Travel</h4>
    <ul style="padding-left:30px">
        <li style="padding-bottom:20px">State College Regional Airport (SCE): Located about 5 miles from the Penn State campus (a roughly 10-minute drive). It offers non-stop flights to Charlotte Douglas (CLT), Chicago (ORD), Philadelphia (PHL), and Washington D.C. (IAD) via American Airlines and United Airlines. Ground transport includes local taxis, rideshares, and car rentals. For more information, see here: <a href="https://www.flysce.com/">https://www.flysce.com/</a></li>
        <li>Harrisburg Airport (MDT): Located about 100 miles from the Penn State campus (is roughly 1 hour and 40 minutes drive). Destinations that the State College Regional Airport does not offer are: Atlanta, Boston, Dallas, Detroit (via American Airlines, United Airlines, and Delta) and various destinations of budget airline Allegiant.</li>
    </ul>
<br>
<h4>Bus and Train Travel</h4>
    <ul style="padding-left:30px">
        <li style="padding-bottom:20px">Bus Services: Greyhound and Fullington Trailways service the State College bus terminal just west of campus on North Atherton Street, with additional connections via FLiXBUS.</li>
        <li>Train Services: Amtrak does not stop directly in State College; the nearest stations are in Lewistown (east) or Tyrone (west), which require an additional rideshare or shuttle connection to reach campus.</li>
    </ul>
