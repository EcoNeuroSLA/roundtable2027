<h3>Call For Papers</h3>
<p>We are inviting submissions for 15-minute oral presentations or poster presentations. Title and abstract (between 400-500 words, excluding references) should be submitted through <a href="https://easyabs.linguistlist.org/conference/EcoNeuroSLA/">Easy Abs</a>, by February 1, 2027, 11:59pm (anywhere on earth). Decisions will be made by February 21. Note that Easy Abs will require a free account to submit.</p>
<br>
<p>We expect the speakers to present studies that identify, innovate, or employ methods that will help increase the ecological validity of research addressing adult second/additional language learning and processing. Based on their abstract, speakers may be invited to submit their work to a special issue in Language Learning’s Cognitive Neuroscience Series that will be published towards the end of 2028.</p>
